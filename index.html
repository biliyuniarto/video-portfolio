02 / Approach

Strategy before
the timeline.

Good-looking footage is only raw material. I start with what the audience needs to understand or feel, then let that goal shape the hook, coverage, edit, and platform treatment.

  1. 01

    Find the reason

    Translate the brief into one clear audience problem, need, or curiosity worth stopping for.

  2. 02

    Plan the coverage

    Build the shots, talent beats, product details, and proof points around the final story.

  3. 03

    Edit for clarity

    Structure the message first, then use pacing, text, sound, and visual rhythm to hold attention.

  4. 04

    Fit the platform

    Adapt the delivery to the screen, context, and viewing behavior instead of reposting blindly.
