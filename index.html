02 / Approach

The edit starts
before the camera rolls.

Good-looking footage is only the raw material. The real work is deciding what the viewer should feel, understand, and do next.

  1. 01

    Find the hook

    Translate the brief into one sharp audience reason to keep watching.

  2. 02

    Design the coverage

    Plan frames, movement, talent beats, and product moments around the final cut.

  3. 03

    Build the rhythm

    Edit for clarity first, then pace, sound, text, and retention.

  4. 04

    Fit the platform

    Shape each delivery for its screen, context, and audience behavior.
